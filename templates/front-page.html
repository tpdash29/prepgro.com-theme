    Your child takes a free adaptive check — about 20 minutes, one question per screen, save and resume any time. You get the readiness report the same day, written for parents, not psychometricians.

  2. 2Elevate the plan

    Full-length mocks under real timing show the plan is working. When a gap won’t move on its own, that’s the moment for a matched expert session — targeted at the gap, not generic tutoring hours.

How readiness is measured

Diagnostics and mocks mirror each exam’s real structure and timing. Readiness reflects accuracy, coverage of the exam’s skill areas, and how recent the evidence is — and reports always say what the number means.

Your child’s data stays theirs

Student results are visible to the student and their parent — never on public pages, never sold. Anything marked “sample” on this site is illustrative, not a real student. Read the privacy policy.

No score guarantees

No honest program can promise a specific score. We promise clarity instead: where your child stands, what to work on next, and proof over time that it’s working.

Questions before you begin?

How much time does the readiness check take?
About 20 minutes. It’s adaptive, one question per screen, and your child can save and resume. The report is ready as soon as they finish.
Which exams and grades does PrepGro cover?
SAT, ACT, PSAT, AP subjects, and state assessments across all 50 states, plus school subjects for grades 3–12. Use the chooser above or browse the full practice-test library.
Can my child work independently?
Yes — the weekly plan is designed for students to run on their own: short sets, one clear next step, and explanations for every question. You stay informed through the parent digest instead of standing over their shoulder.
When does an expert make sense?
When a specific gap isn’t moving with practice alone, or the timeline is short. Matching starts from your child’s diagnostic data, and every session ends with a recap and an updated plan.
What do you do with my child’s data?
Results are used to build the report and the plan — that’s it. They’re visible to your family and never shown publicly or sold. See the privacy policy for the full details.
How does billing work?
The readiness check is free with no credit card. Paid plans list exactly what’s included on the pricing page, and you can cancel anytime.
