FAQ

Good questions

How long is the readiness check?

About 20 minutes. One question per screen, and you can save and resume. The report is ready as soon as it is finished.

What does unlimited practice include?

Unlimited mock practice tests for one selected subject, timed or untimed, with answer explanations, review and skill-level progress tracking.

How many live classes come with a tutor plan?

Eight live 1:1 classes each month, plus the Unlimited Test Pack for the enrolled subject.

Can we add a second subject?

Yes. One subject is included in each subscription; add another subject plan whenever you need it.

Do you guarantee a score?

No. Practice tests are designed to help students prepare; prepGro does not guarantee examination scores.
